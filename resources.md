# 🧠⚡️ Welcome to the Computational Neuroscience Resources Repository ⚡️🧠

Salam AiN Family! 👋

Following the summer school, we put together this collection of FREE resources to support your learning in computational neuroscience.

This is not a structured curriculum, and we do not expect anyone to work through every resource. Instead, use the sections most relevant to the skills you want to develop, and let them guide you toward what to learn next.

Our goal is to support consistent, practical learning. Whenever possible, learn by applying what you study to real problems, datasets, and projects.

## Cross-Skill 🐍➕🧠➕📈

Computational neuroscience is built on connecting the dots!

Here's where disciplines meet:

- [**Neuromatch Academy: CN**](https://compneuro.neuromatch.io/tutorials/intro.html) is the ultimate summer school experience, even if you're just browsing their materials.

### Core Textbooks/Courses

- [**Theoretical Neuroscience**](https://github.com/SWARAJkumar/Computational-Neuroscience/blob/master/Theoretical%20Neuroscience%20Computational%20and%20Mathematical%20Modeling%20of%20Neural%20Systems%20-%20%20Peter%20Dayan%2C%20L.%20F.%20Abbott.pdf) is where you end up for a professional start, should you ever wish to systematically approach CN as a discipline.
- [**Neuronal Dynamics**](https://neuronaldynamics.epfl.ch/online/) is where you go if you want an accessible version.
- [**Computational Cognitive Neuroscience**](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLu02O8xRZn7xtNx03Rlq6xMRdYcQgEpar) if you liked the tutorials.
- Totally lost? Some more motivation to learn CN? [**BT6270**](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLqFGS_otF-c5SZ4XPaR2sUNq5EKCKnyGJ)

### Some Neuro-Specific Python Skills (Check Examples/Tutorials for Packages)

- Study [**MNE-Python**](https://mne.tools/stable/index.html) as the gold standard for EEG & MEG analysis.
- Check out [**SpikeInterface**](https://spikeinterface.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html) to learn about spike sorting.
- Explore [**Elephant**](https://elephant.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) to learn more about spike trains.
- Study [**Nilearn**](https://nilearn.github.io/) to handle fMRI from preprocessing to visualization.
- Look at [**BrainPy**](https://brainpy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) for a go-to for simulating neurons and networks.
- Work on the next level with [**Nengo**](https://www.nengo.ai) and build large-scale cognitive models.

### Some More Useful Statistics

- When in doubt, explore the **[Springer Texts in Statistics](https://www.springer.com/series/0417)**.
  - Notably, [**An Introduction to Statistical Learning**](https://www.statlearning.com)

### Practice Beyond Theory

The most important advice is to learn by practice, ideally while working on a project. Find basic and neuroscience datasets, go through them and try to replicate the findings, and try to do one more thing than the original paper, or something different than theirs, and see where things take you.

- Learn about the **[NWB Data Format](https://www.nwb.org)**, as standardization is key!
- [**Open Neuroscience**](https://open-neuroscience.com) is your hub for neuro tools and datasets
- [**IDA**](https://ida.loni.usc.edu/login.jsp) is also great to find some of the most reliable datasets
- [**brainlife**](https://brainlife.io/about/) is the world’s neuroscience analysis platform.
- [**E-BRAINS**](https://ebrains.eu/) explores brain simulation tools and data
